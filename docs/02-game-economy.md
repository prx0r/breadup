# 02 — Game Economy

## Principle

BreadUp should make users *feel* progress while teaching real economic concepts. The game layer summarizes economics; it never replaces them.

## Bread credits

MVP Bread is a **closed-loop app credit**. It is useful for:

- revealing premium intel
- running scanners
- extending scan frequency
- buying strategy templates
- entering simulated leagues
- customizing agent identities
- unlocking advanced analytics trials

Bread is not marketed as an investment, profit share, deposit, or redeemable claim.

## Earning Bread

Bread can be earned through:

- onboarding missions
- verified referrals
- publishing scanners that users actually consume
- winning simulated leagues
- completing education missions
- contributing validated compatibility/product data
- reporting false scanner positives
- verified real-world outcomes, with caps to avoid pay-to-win loops

Referral Bread should use delayed vesting, device/account reputation, and reversal on fraud.

## Missions that encode real business concepts

### First Bread
**UX:** Earn your first positive realized contribution profit.

**Economic condition:** `realized_contribution_profit > 0` after fees, shipping, refunds, and acquisition cost.

### Turn It
**UX:** Convert inventory back into cash quickly.

**Condition:** a completed sale with `days_held <= cohort_p25_days_held` and positive net profit.

### Margin Callout
**UX:** Improve the unit economics of an existing product.

**Condition:** trailing 20-order contribution margin improves by at least a configurable percentage without increasing refund rate beyond tolerance.

### Cashflow Streak
**UX:** Keep the machine producing.

**Condition:** positive realized contribution profit on N consecutive measurement periods with minimum transaction count.

### De-Risked
**UX:** Recover the original capital from a business.

**Condition:** cumulative cash distributions >= initial contributed capital while remaining inventory is non-negative and liabilities are reconciled.

### Market Maker
**UX:** Create useful intelligence others pay for.

**Condition:** scanner reaches minimum independent buyers, paid calls, and quality threshold.

## Seasons and leagues

Leagues make performance comparable by standardizing:

- starting capital
- allowed data sources
- allowed marketplaces
- maximum spend per action
- time window
- whether execution is simulated or real
- latency assumptions
- transaction fees
- inventory constraints

Recommended leaderboard metrics:

1. realized net P&L
2. return on time-weighted capital
3. maximum drawdown
4. capital turnover
5. evidence quality

Do not rank only by gross revenue or raw ROI.

## Addictive but non-deceptive mechanics

Good mechanics:

- scan streaks
- scanner level-ups based on verified sample size
- live opportunity inbox
- limited-time freshness windows on genuinely expiring intel
- badge collections
- public verified track records
- follow/fork scanner graphs
- missions and seasons
- progressive reveal of advanced metrics
- "one tap to inspect the math"

Avoid:

- paid random loot boxes
- fake countdowns
- fabricated scarcity
- token-price celebration as product progress
- hiding realized losses behind XP
- variable-ratio monetary rewards disguised as gameplay

## Reveal mechanic

A premium scanner may emit a teaser:

- category
- marketplace
- confidence bucket
- estimated net edge bucket
- freshness
- scanner track record

The precise listing/source can be purchased with Bread or x402. The user is paying for **specific information access**, not a random chance to receive something valuable.
