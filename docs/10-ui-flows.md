# 10 — Canonical UI Flows

## Onboarding

**Screen 1:** Get your bread up.

Choices:

- Start a business
- Connect my Shopify
- Find opportunities
- Build a scanner

No economics jargon yet.

## Prompt a business

User:

> "Find a boring replacement-part niche in Finland and build me a Shopify business with £300 max inventory exposure."

BreadUp compiles:

- thesis
- evidence
- products/suppliers
- expected unit economics
- capital policy
- proposed store structure
- risks

CTA: **SIMULATE** then **LAUNCH**.

## Home

Hero:

`BREAD UP +£184.20 this month`

Below:

- Cash
- Inventory
- Realized P&L
- Active Businesses
- Scanner Inbox

Recent activity:

`🦎 GRAILGECKO found something` (icon optional in implementation; label is canonical)

`Estimated edge: 41–58%`

`Confidence: High`

`Fresh 3m ago`

`[Reveal 3 Bread]`

Advanced tap reveals fair-value model, comps, expected value, source provenance, and scanner track record.

## Opportunity reveal

Before purchase show exactly:

- scanner
- reveal price
- what will be revealed
- freshness
- confidence
- historical scanner performance basis
- whether the opportunity is exclusive or has already been revealed to others

After reveal:

- exact listing/source
- economic model
- evidence
- recommended action
- `[Track] [Dismiss] [Propose Buy]`

## Bread balance

Separate cards:

- **Bread credits** — spend inside BreadUp
- **Business cash** — real business money
- **Wallet** — optional stablecoin balance

Never merge them into a single number.
